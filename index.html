The Interval Between未及之間

Galerie Pierre, Taichung臻品藝術中心, 台中

20 December 2025 – 13 February 20262025.12.20 – 2026.02.13

Exhibition →展覽 →

Julia Hung works with enamelled copper wire, discarded plastics and ordinary objects, using techniques drawn from domestic labour — cooking, ironing, weaving — to build biomorphic forms that hold a material somewhere between solid and fluid.洪郁雯以彩漆銅線、廢棄塑料與日常物件創作，援引烹煮、熨燙、編織等家務勞動的技術，構築介於固態與流動之間的生物形態。
