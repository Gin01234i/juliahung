Indefinite Pink

2024

Material
Mixed media
Dimensions
H400 × W400 × D600 cm
Shown
TAIPEI DANGDAI 2024

Composed of strings bound in soft translucent resin rather than woven, Indefinite Pink transforms gestures historically associated with textile labor into a semi-transparent architectural form that appears both fragile and unstable. Balancing monumentality and ephemerality, the installation reflects on the instability of cultural symbols and the shifting conditions through which meaning is formed and perceived.
