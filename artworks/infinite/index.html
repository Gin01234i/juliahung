Infinite

2022

Material
Enamelled copper wire
Series
Infinite
Dimensions
H178 × W90 × D90 cm

Commisioned for Hotel Indigo Alishan

A suspended, rotating sculpture in enameled copper wire, Infinite draws from Buddhist notions of boundlessness, where accumulation unfolds without fixed origin or end. Informed by the shifting seasons of Alishan, the work forms a continuously evolving structure of color and movement.

Operating within a non-linear temporality, the work considers how value—material, perceptual, and immaterial—is not contained, but remains in circulation, continuously transforming.
