Untamed

Untamed considers the idea of an original state, questioning the extent to which it can exist outside systems of control. The work reflects on the tension between imposed structure and unregulated formation.

Developed without predeterminated composition, asymmetrical forms emerge intuitively, allowing material, light, and shadow to remain in flux. Rather than proposing a return to an untouched state, the work holds open the question of whether such a condition is possible—or already shaped by the very frameworks it seeks to resist.
