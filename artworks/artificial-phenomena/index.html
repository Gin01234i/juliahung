Artifical Phenomena

Through intuitive weaving in copper wire, Julia Hung explores interdependence and transformation, drawing from Taoist thought where apparent opposites remain mutually constitutive. Organic forms emerge from a material often associated with rigidity, shifting between structure and fluidity, light and shadow—where tension and care coexist, and moments of quiet resilience take form.
