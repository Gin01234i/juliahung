Through woven copper wire, projected light, and shadow, Mystic ⽞ (Xuan) explores unstable states between presence  and  absence.  The  work洪郁雯以有機繪畫性的銅線編織系列「Mystic 玄」，來探討了顯性和隱性、存在與虛無間的流動性。

continuously appears and dissolves, allowing visibility and invisibility to emerge as interdependent conditions shaped through perception.

Julia Hung's organic and drawing-like copper wire weaving series "Mystic 玄 (Xuan)" explores the fluidity between the visible and invisible, and between being and nothingness.

在道家思想中，「無」與「有」說法不同，其實來源相同而名稱各異，為「玄」。有和無看似相反，其實相生互換，猶如白隱於白，顯於黑，反之亦然。

透過黑白色系及光影的運用，藝術家將作品型態，重新定義且溶解於空間，流動於可見和不可見之間，也體現存在與虛無的共存本質，突顯了感知是如何不斷被塑造和重塑的。
