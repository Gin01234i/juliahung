Being as a Woman

2025

Material
Enamelled copper wire
Dimensions
H35 × W21 × D15 cm
Shown
The Interval Between

Utilizing an aesthetic of ethereal fragility, this suspended sculpture transforms repetitive gestures associated with domestic labor into biomorphic forms that hover between organic flow and physical containment. The work investigates femininity not as an innate condition, but as something constructed, performed, and continually redefined.
