21g

2024

Material
Enamelled copper wire
Dimensions
9 × 3 × 3 m (size varies)
Shown
Voices

Referencing mythological threads of fate and early experiments attempting to measure the weight of the soul, 21g explores humanity’s fixation on quantification and standardization. The installation reflects on what remains immeasurable within contemporary systems of control and measurement.
