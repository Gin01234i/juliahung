Déjà Vu

2022–

Material
Enamelled copper wire
Series
Déjà Vu series
Dimensions
H135 × W60 × D72 cm
Shown
The Interval Between

Through intuitive weaving processes developed without fixed composition, Déjà Vu explores the relationship between free will, repetition, and nonlinear time. The suspended copper wire forms emerge through accumulated gestures that feel simultaneously spontaneous and predetermined, evoking a sense of familiarity that resists clear origin.
