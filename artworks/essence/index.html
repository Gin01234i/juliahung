Essence series

2022

Material
Enamelled copper wire
Dimensions
H130 × W113 × D38 cm
Shown
2022 台中藝術博覽會

Essence traces shifting states of matter through woven copper wire. Condensed forms descend into fluid movement, dispersing into cloud-like extensions that hover between density and diffusion. Rather than fixed states, the work unfolds through continuous transformation—where solidity and permeability, containment and release, remain mutually constitutive and interchangeable.
